How should we choose the first experiment?

Choose a specific task with available anonymised samples, an accountable reviewer and agreed acceptance criteria. Test completeness, evidence and exception handling before connecting business systems. Output counts do not replace value validation.

Choose a service format

  • Half-day Salon: develop AI understanding and identify useful questions through scenario discussions.
  • One-day Skill Workshop: build a reusable skill and workflow around one problem or technique.
  • Two-day, one-night Hackathon: co-create an MVP, demonstrate it and validate key capabilities.
  • Five-day BootCamp: validate actual functionality against agreed needs and produce outputs ready for further deployment work; unconditional launch is not promised.
  • Three-month Organisational Support: combine workshops and project consulting to review progress and advance business applications.

Common questions

Does a healthcare AI workshop mean a production launch?

No. Workshops may produce clearer requirements, workflows, MVPs or functionality-validation records. Deployment requires further confirmation of functionality, permissions, data and operating conditions against agreed criteria.

Can non-technical staff participate?

Yes. Workshops serve non-technical learners, business leaders and department heads. Activities start with business problems and existing materials; tools and task complexity are chosen for participants and acceptance goals.

Does an unsuccessful experiment still produce an outcome?

Failure is allowed. Identifying infeasible approaches, capability limits, missing materials and clearer requirements can be stage outcomes. Acceptance criteria are agreed before the workshop.

How can we discuss collaboration?

Prepare your business question, participating departments, available anonymised materials and validation goals, then open the Feishu enquiry form from the homepage consultation section. Your request is saved in a restricted table for the team to follow up.
