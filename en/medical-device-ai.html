Medical device AI solutions and workshops

Medical device AI solutions begin with a specific business problem. Industry and AI specialists work together to test scenarios, functionality and feasibility in workshops before deciding whether further investment is justified.

Jie’s AI Lab brings together its founder, industry specialists, AI specialists and project partners for project-based workshops and consulting. Rooted in healthcare technology, it serves non-technical learners, business leaders and department heads across industries through problem discovery, experiments and value validation.

Which business scenarios can be tested first?

Potential tasks include production and supply chains, policy access and market intelligence, expenses and decision analysis, and collaboration and management automation. These areas appear in the Weigao workshop record; each solution still depends on company workflows, data permissions and acceptance needs.

Choose a service format

  • Half-day Salon: develop AI understanding and identify useful questions through scenario discussions.
  • One-day Skill Workshop: build a reusable skill and workflow around one problem or technique.
  • Two-day, one-night Hackathon: co-create an MVP, demonstrate it and validate key capabilities.
  • Five-day BootCamp: validate actual functionality against agreed needs and produce outputs ready for further deployment work; unconditional launch is not promised.
  • Three-month Organisational Support: combine workshops and project consulting to review progress and advance business applications.

Common questions

Does this offer medical diagnosis or certified device products?

This page describes business-workflow experiments and training. It makes no claim of medical diagnosis services, certified devices or clinical outcomes. Business and AI specialists define tasks together, with human review and conditions for further pilots.

Can non-technical staff participate?

Yes. Workshops serve non-technical learners, business leaders and department heads. Activities start with business problems and existing materials; tools and task complexity are chosen for participants and acceptance goals.

Does an unsuccessful experiment still produce an outcome?

Failure is allowed. Identifying infeasible approaches, capability limits, missing materials and clearer requirements can be stage outcomes. Acceptance criteria are agreed before the workshop.

How can we discuss collaboration?

Prepare your business question, participating departments, available anonymised materials and validation goals, then visit the homepage consultation section. The Feishu registration link is not yet available and its button remains disabled; registration will be possible once it opens.
